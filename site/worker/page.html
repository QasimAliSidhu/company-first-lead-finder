How to update your lead database

Run your local company finder, then choose Import companies and select data/enriched_companies.json. Imported records stay in this browser; the published database remains available using “Use published database.” CSV export follows your filters and keeps one row per company/email.

Company discovery uses OpenSERP, Google Maps, and business directories (Yelp, Yellow Pages, Clutch, and GoodFirms), followed by public website enrichment, domain deduplication, and configurable scoring. New nationwide searches run in your local collector. This dashboard displays a saved dataset; changing filters does not discover new companies. The local collector supports multiple cities, deeper Maps discovery and Crawl4AI browser enrichment. Directory profiles can supply business websites and publicly published contacts. When a profile cannot be fetched, indexed directory listings can seed a company website search; those results remain marked for identity and location review. Filter by Discovery source to inspect directory leads. Import a new result to expand your database.
